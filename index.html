04 · Selected work

Products, shipped.

Client Delivery Team Everest NGO

Centralize — OCR & Document Intelligence

A centralized platform that extracts handwritten scholarship forms into a database, replacing fully manual data entry for an NGO running scholarship programmes.

Read the case study
Problem

Team Everest's volunteers hand-fill scholarship verification forms, then staff re-type every one into a database — slow, repetitive and error-prone.

What I built

An OCR pipeline that pulls new uploads from Zoho Creator and extracts handwritten data into Supabase, grown into a centralized app with any-form templates, batch upload, per-document status, Excel export, confidence scoring and Admin/User RBAC.

Outcome

Batches grew from 10–15 files to 108; 100 images process in under 8 minutes. Validated on a GCP VM, documented and handed over in Nov 2026.
